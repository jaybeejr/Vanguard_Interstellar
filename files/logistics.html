MINING AND SALVAGE CALCULATOR

Tip: press Enter or Tab to move to the next box. New rows appear on their own as you type, so you can fill everything in without clicking. Enter what you hauled and the current price per SCU. For mining, also enter how many SCU the refinery takes from each material; you are paid only for the SCU left after that loss. Prices change with each patch and location, so use the Check price link under each mining price to open that material on SC Trade Tools, then type the price in. Nothing here is saved unless you press Save to payout log.
